# Milestones

Milestones mark how far you have come in one workspace: "1,000 reviews", "a 7-day streak", "HSK 1 complete". Each one is shown to you once, in a card, and then kept in a list. There are no notifications, emails, sounds or animations.

## Which milestones there are

| Milestone | Steps | What counts |
|---|---|---|
| Reviews | 100, 500, 1,000, 2,500, 5,000, 10,000, 25,000, 50,000, 100,000 | every flashcard review you graded, also of cards you deleted since |
| Words studied | 10, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000, 2,500, 5,000, 10,000 | different words with a flashcard in one of your decks that you have reviewed at least once |
| Mature words | 10, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000, 2,500, 5,000 | words whose flashcard is mature (in review with a stability of 21 days or more, as on the decks view) |
| Characters learned | 10, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000, 2,000, 3,000, 5,000 | Chinese and Japanese only: characters with a score of 80 of 100 or more, as on the character map |
| Streak | 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 100, 180, 365, 500, 730, 1,000 days | days in a row with a flashcard review, a finished grammar lesson, a drill answer or a course lesson or test; vacation days neither break nor extend it |
| Days active | 10, 30, 50, 100, 200, 365, 500, 1,000 | days on which you reviewed, read for at least 30 seconds, wrote in your journal, finished a grammar lesson, answered a drill or did a course lesson or test; vacation days are left out |
| Reading | 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000 hours | time read in the Reader (as on the heatmap: a stretch under 30 seconds counts nothing, at most 2 hours per stretch) |
| Journal entries | 1, 10, 25, 50, 100, 250, 365, 500 | different journal entries you wrote |
| Level complete | one per level | every word of the level has a mature flashcard, or every character of a character level is learned. Words you only marked as known do not count |
| First course lesson | one per course | the first lesson you finish in the [course](Courses) |
| Unit done | one per unit | a course unit you finished yourself: its unit test passed (also early), or, for a unit without a test, its last lesson. Units skipped by a later test, an exam or placement get none |
| Section done | one per section | every unit of a course section done, skipped units included |
| Script complete | one per script | every unit of the course that teaches the script (hiragana, katakana) done |
| Course characters | 100, 250, 500, 1,000 | the characters of the course units you have done |
| Course words | 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000, 1,500, 2,000, 3,000 | words you met in course lessons, plus the words of units you skipped (as the roadmap counts them) |
| Course complete | one per course | every unit of the course done |

Each milestone is reached on the day you first got there, in your own time zone. It is **ever reached**: if a word later lapses and you drop back to 499 mature words, "500 mature words" stays. Milestones belong to one workspace: your Chinese and your Japanese workspace each have their own.

Milestones follow your feature switches (Settings → Features): with flashcards switched off there are no review, word, character, streak or level milestones; with the Reader off no reading milestones; with the Journal plugin off no journal milestones; with Courses off no course milestones. **Days active** is always there. Switching a feature back on can bring a card with what you reached meanwhile.

## The card

When you reach a milestone, a card appears at the top of the home page and of **Stats → Week**:

- "Milestone reached" names up to three new milestones from the last seven days, each with its date.
- "You have reached 14 milestones" appears instead when there are more, or older ones — for example the first time you open qinqin after this feature arrived, or after a long time offline. It names the three newest and links to the full list (**See all milestones**).

**Dismiss** (or following the link) marks every milestone on the card as seen, also the ones it did not name; the card then goes away for good. Nothing else marks a milestone: scrolling past or opening the tab does not.

The home page counts reviews, words studied, streak, days active, reading, journal entries and the course milestones of your workspace's course. Mature words, characters learned and complete levels need more work to count, so they first appear on **Stats → Week**.

## The list

**Stats → Week** has a **Milestones** section (`/stats/week#milestones`; with Stats switched off the home card has no **See all milestones** link): every milestone you have reached, newest first, the title as a plain row and the date you reached it as muted text. Ones you have not seen yet say **New**. The week's summary also has a **Milestone this week** line with the milestones of the week shown; it follows the **Previous week** and **Next week** arrows.

## Offline, other devices and your data

- Seen milestones are saved on the server and sync to your other devices, so a card you dismissed on your phone does not come back on your laptop. If two devices show the same card before either is dismissed, you may see it once on each.
- Offline, a dismiss is kept in this browser and sent when you are back online. In a private window that blocks storage, an offline dismiss is forgotten when you reload.
- What you have seen is part of [your data](Your-data): it is in the download and comes back with a restore.
