## What

- **The test assembly** (`lib/features/courses/tests.ts`, pure, seeded): a unit test and a section exam are a mix of
  **word** questions (meaning, form, listening with a device voice, and how the word is read), **grammar** questions
  (fill the gap, from the unit's or section's grammar lessons) and **text** questions (fill the gap in a sentence of
  the unit's reading text). The question count is the pack's `checkpoints.unit/section.questions`.
- **Built on the level check (#82):** the word questions are sampled with the level check's planner
  (`lib/placement/levelCheck.ts` `planCheck`): stratified over the unit's word lessons (a unit test) or the section's
  units (an exam), seeded with the placement test's generator, words the app already calls known (the shared
  predicate, `lib/studycore/known.ts`) left out first.
- **Scoring and judging:** score = right first answers of all questions; passed when `score · 100 ≥ passPercent ·
  total` (a test with nothing to ask passes); recorded as `unit-test {early}` / `section-exam` events (#221).
- **Kind feedback:** a failed test shows the pass mark, what was missed (the words with their meaning, the grammar
  points, the units to go back to, with links to their open lessons), never takes progress away (failed events change
  nothing, #221), and offers **Try again** (a new seed).
- **Placement** ("Find your place", #74): a chain of section exams from the first section with an exam; each
  placement exam stops as soon as it is decided (the placement probe's early stop); a pass moves to the next section's
  exam (or "Start here"), a fail places the learner into that section (`placement {section}`).
- **Contract** `docs/contracts/courses/tests.json` (rule `courses/tests`, web): the budget split, the reading
  question's tone variants, the stratified word sample, the text gap rule, judging, the early stop, the placement
  chain; ContractVersion 31; CHANGELOG.
- **Screens:** the player's test/exam/placement views, the summary's review list and Try again, the roadmap's "Find
  your place"; DOM tests; e2e (placement on a phone) and the new paths in `phone-width.spec.ts`.

## Rulings

Format: `Ruling: <what> — <why> — <cost if wrong>`.

### Assembly

1. Ruling: **the budget split:** with `Q` questions, grammar gets `min(G, ceil(Q/5))` (G = available gap items, one
   per grammar point first, round robin), the text `min(T, ceil(Q/5))` (T = text gap items), the words the rest, at
   most two questions per word (as the #222 quiz); a unit with neither grammar nor text gives all `Q` to its words;
   when the words cannot fill their share the test is shorter (never padded with repeats beyond two per word) — the
   issue asks for "a short mixed test of the unit's words, grammar and reading"; words are most of a unit, a fifth
   each keeps grammar and reading present without crowding the words out — cost if wrong: a different mix is a change
   of two constants and the fixture.
2. Ruling: **the word sample is the level check's `planCheck`**, each word lesson of the unit (a unit test) or each unit
   of the section (an exam) one stratum, `seed` the test's, size the word share; words the shared predicate calls
   known (a mark or a well-reviewed card) are left out; when fewer unproven words than the share remain, a second
   `planCheck` with an empty index over the proven ones fills the rest; a second question on a word comes only after
   every sampled word has one — "built on the level check, not beside it": the same stratified, seeded sampler and the
   same rule that the app's own data needs no question; a learner testing out of a unit they half know from the
   dictionary is asked about what they have not shown — cost if wrong: a learner whose cards are all well reviewed is
   asked about them anyway (only when nothing else is left).
3. Ruling: **word question types rotate** meaning → form → reading → listen (listen only with a device voice; reading
   only when the word has a reading the learner's romanisation setting shows and the language has a reading
   question); a type that does not apply to a word falls to the next — the #222 types plus one, spread evenly —
   cost if wrong: none.
4. Ruling: **the reading question:** Chinese asks the word's pinyin among tone variants of it (the same syllables with
   other tones, built with `lib/exercises/tones` `parsePinyin`/`markedSyllable`, deterministic for the seed); Japanese
   asks the kana reading of a word written with kanji among the readings of other course words; other languages and
   words without a reading get none; with pinyin hidden (romanisation "never", #284) Chinese gets none — tones are
   what Chinese learners confuse and pinyin variants need no other words; Japanese readings of kana-only words are the
   word itself — cost if wrong: a word whose pinyin has a neutral tone gets variants that look odd (the fourth tone
   for a neutral one).
5. Ruling: **grammar questions are the grammar lessons' own gap exercises** (grammar's `buildExercises` cloze items of
   the unit's or section's available grammar points, loaded through grammar's lesson data like the grammar lesson
   step), one per point in turn; unavailable points give none — no new content (this builder writes none); the shipped
   exercises are reviewed content — cost if wrong: a unit whose grammar pack is not installed has no grammar part.
6. Ruling: **text questions are gaps in the unit's reading text:** the text is cut into sentences (after `。！？!?` and
   line breaks); a sentence gives a gap for the first unit word (teaching order) that occurs in it literally
   (`lib/exercises/cloze` `blankSpans` with no segmenter matches, so it works offline) when at least two code points of
   letters stay outside the gap, with three wrong choices picked by `pickDistractors` from the unit's, then the
   section's, then the course's word forms (none that occurs in the sentence); a sentence without three wrong choices
   gives none; each word at most once — reading in context from the project's own texts, without writing questions
   (no content here) and without the server — cost if wrong: a literal match inside a longer word (中国 in 中国人)
   blanks part of a word; the course texts are short and written for the unit, so it is rare.
7. Ruling: **an exam's grammar and text items come from every unit of the section,** a unit test's from its own unit
   — same rules at section scale (issue) — none.
8. Ruling: **the order of a test:** words, grammar and text interleaved by a seeded shuffle, no question asked twice
   in a row about the same word — a mixed test, not three blocks — none.

### Judging and feedback

9. Ruling: **one judge for every test:** `passed = total = 0 or score · 100 ≥ passPercent · total`, score = right
   first answers, `passPercent` the pack's mark when the test is taken (#221 ruling 21) — the existing #222 rule and
   the derivation's history-safe `passed` — none.
10. Ruling: **no retry inside a test** (a wrong answer shows the right one and moves on), and **Try again** starts a new
    test with a new seed — a test is a judgement, the lesson check is the practice — none.
11. Ruling: **what to review** after a test (passed or not): the missed words (form, reading through
    `useRomanisation`, meaning), the missed grammar points (with the Grammar page link), and for an exam the units
    with misses, each with a link to its practice lesson when that lesson is open — "a failed test shows what to
    review"; locked lessons get no link, the words list is still shown — cost if wrong: none.
12. Ruling: **an early test is still `early` as #222 judged it** (any lesson of the unit not done) — the contract's
    meaning — none.

### Placement

13. Ruling: **placement is a chain of section exams**, from the first section that has an exam; each is recorded as a
    `section-exam` (a pass skips the section and all before it, #221); a fail records `placement {section}` of the
    failed section, except a fail of the **first** exam, which places the learner at the course's first section
    (nothing skipped: the start section's sounds and script steps stay); "Start here" after a pass records `placement`
    into the next section; passing the last exam ends the chain — "Placement (#74) reuses the section exams to place a
    learner"; a passed exam already skips, so the placement event marks where the learner starts — cost if wrong: a
    learner who knows the sounds but fails the first exam meets the start section again (it is short).
14. Ruling: **a placement exam stops as soon as its result is decided** (the placement probe's rule, `placement.ts`:
    the pass mark reached, or out of reach), and the event records the questions asked — placement is a tour through
    up to four exams; a learner who misses most of the first questions should not answer the rest — cost if wrong:
    a placement exam's score covers fewer questions than a normal exam's.
15. Ruling: **"Find your place" shows on the roadmap while no unit of a section with an exam is done** — afterwards
    the learner has started; the unit tests and section exams still skip ahead — cost if wrong: a learner who did
    one unit and wants placement takes the section exams one by one instead.

### Other

17. Ruling: **routes:** `/courses/play/<course>/section/<section>/placement` (a placement exam); the unit test and
    the exam keep #222's paths — the player already lives under `/courses/play` (no bottom bar) — none.
18. Ruling: **no new written content; UI strings only, in `en.json`** (de/ja catalogues come in #144/#145) — the brief's
    text-heavy slot is not held — none.
