## The bug, on the shipped CC-CEDICT (greedy, reproduced first)

CC-CEDICT gives one character entry per character with every line's reading, in line order, and the segmenter showed
the first: 那 nā (the surname line), 着 zhāo, 得 dé, 打 dá, 看 kān. Greedy longest match merged reached words into
compounds outside the level list (这不｜是, 一个, 中国人, 明天见, 那是) and crossed word boundaries (我｜有时｜间,
学｜得很｜好, 我｜在教｜室).

## What

1. **`segment/zh.json`**: Chinese gets segmentation rules, so it is read on #348's lattice. The rules format grows
   optional keys (ja.json stays byte-identical, its results unchanged):
   - `costs.levelStep`, `costs.unlisted`: a token whose head entry is a direct member of an installed **word** level
     list of the language (HSK) costs `token + min(levelStep × level index, unlisted)`, any other `token + unlisted`;
   - `compounds: "parts"`: a token of two or more characters whose head is unlisted is no edge when its text can be
     read entirely by listed words and function words inside it (不是 → 不｜是, 中国人 → 中国｜人);
   - `functionReadings`: a function word is recognised by its lemma and its reading (CC-CEDICT has no parts of
     speech): 着 is a function word because its entry has the reading zhe; its match shows that reading;
   - `readings`: a short table of common readings where CC-CEDICT's lines mislead (几 jǐ, 长 cháng, …).
2. **The reading of a match** (zh): the function reading; else the table; else the reading whose everyday senses have
   the most glosses (minor senses — surname, variant, "used in", archaic: the sense display rule — count nothing);
   ties to the earliest; else the first.
3. **The head** of an edge (several entries with one surface): a function word's entry, else the listed entry with the
   lowest level, else the ranking's first.
4. **Contract 39** `docs/contracts/segment/`: `zh.json`, `zh-cases.json` (CC-CEDICT lines, a word level list, texts
   with tokens, readings and function flags, with and without the list), README; Go and web decode it; CHANGELOG.
5. **Real data**: a `realdict` test on the shipped CC-CEDICT and `hsk-words`; long-chapter timing (zh Tatoeba, 5,000
   runes, full CC-CEDICT + HSK) before and after.
