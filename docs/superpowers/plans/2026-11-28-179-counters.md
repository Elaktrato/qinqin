# #179 — measure words (量词) and Japanese counters (2026-11-28)

Milestone v0.9.0, phase 5 (plan §3 "Drills framework", §4). Branch `feature/v090-179-counters` from `feature/v0.9.0`
(dd68dc0b, ContractVersion 34). Drills on the framework (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-28-drills-framework.md`,
`docs/contracts/drills/`), after #180 (tone pairs) and #178 (conjugation).

## What (issue and owner decisions)

- **Chinese:** for a noun of the learner's decks, give its measure word (一＿书 → 本), typed through the IME with a
  choice fallback. Common alternatives are also right; 个 as the general fallback is "acceptable but not idiomatic".
  The data is CC-CEDICT's `CL:` notes (already bundled, CC BY-SA 4.0): derived at run time from the installed
  dictionary, nothing new bundled.
- **Japanese:** the counter of a noun (紙 → 枚), and, as **its own drill**, the readings of number + counter with their
  sound changes (一本 いっぽん, 三本 さんぼん, 六本 ろっぽん, 何本 なんぼん, 一人 ひとり, 二十日 はつか …). JMdict lists no
  counter per noun (owner, 2026-11-26), so a **small hand-written counters table** released by the project (CC BY 4.0),
  reviewed by two Japanese teacher agents and an adjudicator, notes in `REVIEW.md` beside it.
- **Scheduling:** drills (owner), not a card kind. Words from the learner's decks; nouns without data are left out.
- **Shared rules** in a contract with fixtures (item keys, reading generation with sound changes, answer checking);
  ContractVersion 35 (34 on the base; the orchestrator renumbers).
- **Phone first:** 360 px without sideways scroll (paths in `phone-width.spec.ts`), hidden romanisation through
  `useRomanisation()`, the Android WebView with the keyboard kept up and IME composition handled.

## The design

### Three drills

| Drill id | Language | Item | Modes | Asks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `measure-words` | Chinese (`zh`, `zh-Hant`) | the noun: `<entryId>` | `produce` (typed), `choose` | 一 ＿ 书 (book): the measure word |
| `counters` | Japanese | the noun: `<entryId>` | `produce`, `choose` | 紙 (paper): the counter |
| `counter-readings` | Japanese | counter × number: `<counterId>:<number>` (`hon:3`, `hon:nan`) | `produce`, `choose` | 3本: its reading (さんぼん) |

### The server (`internal/counters`; contract `docs/contracts/counters/`)

- **CL notes:** a gloss `CL:個|个[ge4],位[wei4]` (or a note `(CL:張|张[zhang1])` inside a gloss) gives measure words
  (traditional|simplified[numbered pinyin]); an entry's measure words are those of every sense that is not minor, in
  order, duplicates once. The shown gloss is the sense display rule's one gloss (`lexicon.DisplayGloss`) with the CL
  notes taken out first (they would give the answer away).
- **个 fallback:** 个 is "acceptable but not idiomatic" for a noun whose measure words do not include 个 and whose first
  one is an individual classifier of the contract's `fallbackAfter` list (本, 张, 只, 条, …; not containers, pairs,
  masses: 杯, 双, 块 of money …).
- **The counters table** (`server/internal/counters/table/counters-ja.json`, CC BY 4.0, `NOTICE`, `REVIEW.md`):
  counters (id, written form and variants, base reading, what it counts, its sound class, numbers asked, overrides),
  and nouns (written, reading, counters, canonical first).
- **Reading generation:** number readings × base reading by sound class (h: 1/6/8/10 → っぷ, 3/何 → ぶ; k, s, t: 1/8/10
  geminate, k also 6; voiced-after-3 flag; native つ), then the table's per-number overrides; canonical first, the
  accepted alternatives after (はちほん? only where reviewed). Plus the **common mistakes** of each item (the
  plain joins and the other sound variants that are not accepted), used as wrong choices.
- **Routes:** `POST /api/drills/measure-words/words {ids}` (1–500): for each Chinese noun with CL data and each
  Japanese noun of the table: written, readings, gloss, measures (canonical first), the fallback, and per language a
  `pool` of other measure words / counters for wrong choices. `GET /api/drills/counters`: the counters with every
  asked number's readings and mistakes. Both signed in, `noTok`, not cached by the service worker.

### The web (`lib/features/practice/counters/`)

- Candidates: the workspace's reviewed word cards' entries, first reviewed first (#178's `studiedEntryIds`), through
  the route; `counter-readings`: every counter × number of the table, in table order.
- Question: a text field (IME composition aware, the field keeps its focus, Enter checks, then moves on), Check,
  "Show choices" (four choices). Typed right = Good, from the choices = Hard, 个 fallback = Hard with the note, wrong =
  Again. Readings: kana always, pinyin, bopomofo and romaji only through `useRomanisation()`.

## Rulings

- Ruling: **no new pack type** (record 0008 names #179 as a possible one) — the Chinese data is derived at request time
  from the installed CC-CEDICT (no copy to keep in step, licence and credit stay the dictionary's), and the Japanese
  table is small project-written data embedded in the server like the Starter lists (`starter/sets`), credited on the
  Sources page through the Practice feature's `sources` — cost if wrong: a later issue that wants user-replaceable
  counter data moves the table into a pack (one migration and the 0008 steps).
- Ruling: **three drills** (`measure-words`, `counters`, `counter-readings`) — a drill title is one catalogue string,
  not per language ("Measure words" vs "Counters"), and the issue wants the readings "as their own drill"; each has its
  own activity row and goal — cost if wrong: three rows where one could do.
- Ruling: **item `<entryId>`** for both noun drills (the drills README's planned shape) and
  **`<counterId>:<number>`** for the readings (`hon:3`, `nichi:20`, `hon:nan` for 何), counter ids ASCII from the
  table — ids are stable when a written form changes (ヶ月/か月) — cost: none.
- Ruling: **words** are the workspace's reviewed word cards' entries, first reviewed first, as #178 — a measure word
  belongs to a noun the learner knows — cost if wrong: a new deck's nouns join after their first review.
- Ruling: **the measure words of an entry** are the CL notes of all its senses that are not minor (sense display rule),
  in sense order — CC-CEDICT puts the note on the line of the reading it belongs to; a card's pinned reading is not
  consulted (rare: 车 jū has no CL) — cost if wrong: a polyphone's other reading's measure word is also right.
- Ruling: **every listed measure word is right** (the first is shown as the answer, the others are "also right") —
  CC-CEDICT lists them as usual alternatives — cost if wrong: a rarer listed one gets Good.
- Ruling: **个 fallback** only after an individual classifier of `fallbackAfter`, graded **Hard** with "acceptable,
  but … is idiomatic" — "一个纸" or "一个水" is not acceptable, "一个苹果" is; Hard counts as right in the round —
  cost if wrong: the list (teacher-reviewed) decides a few nouns.
- Ruling: **typed Chinese** is the character (simplified or traditional, either accepted; a leading number such as
  一本 is ignored) or tone pinyin (`ben3`, `běn`); toneless pinyin is not accepted — it cannot tell 本 from 奔 — cost:
  a learner without tone input types the character.
- Ruling: **typed Japanese counter** is the counter (枚, a variant spelling ヶ月/か月/カ月), its reading in kana (まい)
  or its romaji, a leading number ignored (三枚) — cost: none.
- Ruling: **typed reading** is kana (katakana read as hiragana) or romaji; text with a kanji or a digit is **not
  graded** but answered with "Type the reading in kana" — an IME converts さんぼん to 三本 on Enter, which says nothing
  about the reading — cost: a learner who types 三本 is asked again in kana.
- Ruling: the readings drill asks **1–10 and 何** for every counter, plus the table's extra numbers (20 for 歳 and 日,
  14 and 24 for 日, 11 and 12 for 月) — the sound changes live in 1–10; 何 is the other classic one — cost if wrong:
  larger numbers (百本 ひゃっぽん) are not drilled.
- Ruling: the number is shown in **Arabic numerals** (3本) — horizontal text and phones use them; the answer shows the
  kanji form too (三本) — cost: none.
- Ruling: **wrong choices** of the readings drill are the item's common mistakes (さんほん, さんぽん for 3本), else other
  numbers' readings — the classic slips are what the drill trains — cost: none.
- Ruling: the noun drills' **wrong choices** come from the route's `pool` (common measure words, the other counters),
  never one that is also right for the noun (nor 个 when it is the fallback) — cost: none.
- Ruling: **introduction order** of the readings drill: table order, counter by counter, numbers in order — a counter's
  pattern is learned in one run — cost if wrong: one counter's eleven items come before the next counter.
- Ruling: grades: typed right **Good**, from the choices **Hard**, 个 fallback **Hard**, wrong **Again**; never Easy — the
  framework's "right after a hint" — cost: none.
- Ruling: the IME handling and the kept keyboard are #178's (`conjugation/ime.ts` `submitsAnswer`, the field read-only
  after the answer, never remounted) — cost: none.
- Ruling: ContractVersion **35** (34 on the base; the orchestrator renumbers).
