# Japanese grammar — coverage

The JLPT publishes no grammar list. The selection and order of grammar points per level are
qinqin’s own (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-ja-grammar-pack-design.md), not taken from JLPT
preparation books or other published lists; the levels are estimates. Explanations, examples and
exercises are qinqin’s own.
