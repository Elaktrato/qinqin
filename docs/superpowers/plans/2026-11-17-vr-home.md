> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:subagent-driven-development (fresh implementer

**Goal:** Home in the new look, and the activity heatmap in three layouts each person chooses in
Settings → Home. Home: a greeting heading with the learner's date, the search as an underline field, the
milestones card and the widgets **without boxes** (design §5), in the existing layout system. Heatmap
layouts: **Garden grid** (13 weeks, today's grid), **Month calendar** (dates, bigger tiles, paged by month),
**This week** (seven large days with amounts; tapping opens Stats). The choice is stored in a new user-global
row `qinqin-core` / `widget-options`: `{"version": 1, "activity-heatmap": {"layout": "garden"|"month"|"week"}}`
(default `garden`; unknown keys kept on write), with a contract fixture decoded by Go and web tests. Tiles are
**tinted only** (the flower drawings are the owner's, #107); each day's **flower stage** is computed from the
goals contract and exposed as text (tooltip and accessible name), never drawn.

**Design (binding):** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-visual-rework-design.md` §5, §6 (accent tints), §9
"Home" (layouts, `widget-options`, flower stages), §10 (the row is separate from `home-layout`); mockups
`docs/superpowers/specs/assets/2026-11-16-visual-rework-mockups.html` sections "Decided: the heatmap, three
layouts to choose from" and the home panel inside "Decided: desktop frame A" / "the phone drawer". Direction:
`docs/superpowers/directions/2026-11-17-visual-rework.md` ("the heatmap ships with tint-only tiles"). Goals
rules: `docs/contracts/goals/README.md`.

**Precondition: build on the token-migrated tree and on the frame.** Cut the branch from
`feature/visual-rework` after `feature/vr-tokens` (#98 PART B: every component on tokens, the colour lint) and

**Architecture.**
- `apps/web/src/lib/widgets/options.ts` (new) — the `widget-options` row: `WIDGET_OPTIONS_KEY`,
  `parseWidgetOptions(value)` → `{row, heatmapLayout}` (`row` = the stored object kept for writing),
  `withHeatmapLayout(row, layout)` (the stored object with only `activity-heatmap.layout` replaced; every other
  key — top-level and inside `activity-heatmap` — kept; `version` kept when ≥ 1, else 1),
  `loadWidgetOptions()`, `saveHeatmapLayout(layout)` (reads the row fresh with `find`, applies
  `withHeatmapLayout`, `put`s — so another device's unknown keys written since this page loaded survive).
- `apps/web/src/lib/goals/stage.ts` (new) — `flowerStage(day: GoalDay)` → `'soil'|'sprout'|'bud'|'opening'|
  'bloom'|'resting'|null` (null = no goal → no flower); fixture `docs/contracts/goals/flower-stages.json`.
- `apps/web/src/lib/activity/calendar.ts` (new, pure) — `monthOf(day)`, `monthWindow(month, today)` →
  `{from, to}` (to = min(month end, today)), `monthGrid(month, today)` → weeks Monday-first of
  `{day|null, future}`, `prevMonth`/`nextMonth`, `earliestMonth(today)` (the month of `today - 365`, the
  activity route's 366-day cap), `weekDays(today)` (Monday..Sunday of today's week, future days flagged).
- `apps/web/src/lib/activity/stages.ts` (new, pure) — `stagesFor({row, from, to, today, amountsOf, vacation,
  unitOf})` → `Map<day, {stage, amount, target, metric}>` via `goalDays` + `flowerStage`.
- Components: `ActivityHeatmap.svelte` keeps the data (fetch, scope, type chips, vacations, sequencing) and
  gains a `layout` prop (default `garden`); the three drawings are split into
  `lib/activity/HeatmapGarden.svelte` (today's grid, moved), `HeatmapMonth.svelte`, `HeatmapWeek.svelte`, each
  given `cells` (`{day, level, vacation, today, future, label, stage?, amountText?}`) and rendering tiles plus
  the screen-reader list. `lib/widgets/ActivityHome.svelte` (new) is the widget's `homeComponent`: it loads
  `widget-options` and renders `ActivityHeatmap layout={…}`. Stats keeps `component: ActivityHeatmap`
  (garden).
- Settings → Home: `HomeLayoutEditor.svelte` shows, on the `activity-heatmap` row, a "Heatmap layout" radio
  group (three options with a one-line description each), saved through `saveHeatmapLayout`.
- Home: `routes/+page.svelte` — greeting `h1` and date line, `Field`-style underline search, unboxed widgets
  (`WidgetCard.svelte` loses border/fill; its heading becomes the small sentence-case section heading).
- Contract: `docs/contracts/settings/README.md` + `docs/contracts/settings/widget-options.json` (web
  `lib/widgets/options.test.ts`; Go `server/cmd/qinqin/core_widget_options_test.go`,

## Accessibility (binding)

- The heatmap keeps its pattern: the visual tiles are `aria-hidden`, a visually hidden summary and a list of
  the days with activity (or a stage) carry the meaning — for all three layouts. Each listed day reads e.g.
  "Mon 16 Nov: 21 reviews, 21 of 50 — bud, today".
- Tooltips: tiles keep the existing hover text (now the shared `Tooltip` where a tile is focusable — the week
  layout's link; elsewhere `title` stays on the decorative tile, as today, because the tiles are not focusable
  and the list carries the text).
- Month calendar: Previous / Next month are `IconButton`s (named, tooltips), disabled at `earliestMonth` and
  the current month; the month name is a live heading (`aria-live="polite"` on the month label) so paging is
  announced; the calendar grid itself is decorative (`aria-hidden`, no table roles) — the list is the
  accessible form.
- This week: the whole block is one link to `/stats/week` named "This week — open Stats, Week" (not seven
  links), with a visible focus ring; the day tiles inside are decorative.
- Settings → Home: the layout choice is a `fieldset` with a `legend` and three radio inputs (labels + the
  description linked with `aria-describedby`); a save failure is announced in the editor's existing live region.
- Home: one `h1` (the greeting); widget headings stay `h2`; the search field has a visible or visually hidden
  label (it keeps `app.home.searchLabel`).
- Reduced motion and forced colours: tiles in forced colours draw `CanvasText` outlines and the today ring in
  `Highlight`; no animations are added.

## Rulings

- Ruling: the layout applies to the **home** heatmap only (`homeComponent`); Stats → Overview keeps the garden
  grid — the design places the choice "for their home page in Settings → Home" and Stats is #105's — cost: a
  person who picks Month sees the garden in Stats until #105 decides.
- Ruling: `saveHeatmapLayout` re-reads the row before writing (read-modify-write) instead of writing the copy
  loaded with the page — "unknown keys kept on write" must also hold for keys another device wrote meanwhile
  — cost: one extra GET per change (rare).
- Ruling: a row whose `version` is above 1 is read leniently (its `activity-heatmap.layout` counts when it is
  one of the three) and written back with its version unchanged; a non-object row, a missing or non-integer
  `version`, or an unknown layout reads as `garden` — the `home-layout` and `appearance` precedent — cost: a
  newer client must accept that an older one changes `activity-heatmap.layout` under its version number.
- Ruling: tile tint is the day's activity points (today's `levelFor` levels), not the goal fraction — the
  mockup tints "by how much you did" and the stage is the goal's measure; both are in the text — cost: a day
  far below a big goal can look dark.
- Ruling: stages exist only in the **This workspace** scope with a goal row (goals are per workspace,
  `goals:workspace`); in **All workspaces** every tile is tint-only with no stage text — cost: none (the
  scope switch says which view it is).
- Ruling: stage amounts use the heatmap's own activity response (cells of the current workspace, its
  `sources` for units) plus `loadStudyHistory` (cached: goal row, reviews for `flashcards:*`, vacations) — no
  second activity request; a goal row that fails to load leaves tiles tint-only (the stage is decoration
  until the drawings exist) — cost: a stale cache shows yesterday's stage until the next study write reloads
  it (`onEventLogChange` already reloads the heatmap).
- Ruling: Month calendar pages back to `earliestMonth(today)` (the activity route's 366-day cap) and never
  forward past the current month; it fetches its own month window per page (the 13-week response does not
  cover older months) and shows the current month first — cost: one request per page.
- Ruling: This week shows Monday..Sunday of the learner's current week (the weekly summary's Monday start);
  under each day the goal amount "21/50" when a stage exists, else the day's points; future days are empty
  outlines; the block links to `/stats/week` (Stats → Week, the same week in detail) — the design says
  "tapping opens Stats"; the Week tab is the matching view — cost: one href if the owner meant Overview.
- Ruling: the day number in Month tiles is `--text` on levels 0–2 and `--on-accent` on levels 3–4; the
  visual check measures both per theme and the fix wave adjusts the threshold if any falls below 4.5:1 —
  cost: a theme with a mid-tone accent may need a different split.
- Ruling: Home's `h1` becomes a greeting by the device's hour ("Good morning" < 12, "Good afternoon" < 18,
  else "Good evening") with the learner's today as a date line (`formatDay`); the mock's "day 23 of your
  streak" and "Word of the day" are not added (the streak lives in the daily-goal widget; there is no word of
  the day feature — non-goal "no change to what features do") — cost: Home differs from the mock there.
- Ruling: the home search stays (an underline field with a plain "Search" button) though the bar has a Search
  icon — removing it would change what Home does; on the phone it is the quickest way to search — cost: a
  little more than the mock.
- Ruling: `WidgetCard` is unboxed everywhere (Home and Stats), not only on Home — §5 applies to every page and
  one card component cannot be boxed in one place and not the other without a prop nobody wants — cost:
  Stats changes a little before #105.
- Ruling: the flower stage legend of the mockup is not shown (there are no drawings); stage names appear only
  in tooltips and accessible text — cost: none.
