# qinqin v0.5.0

Study improvements, a Japanese lemmatizer, full grammar packs, a phone bottom bar, and the documentation as a
GitHub wiki. Image: `ghcr.io/elaktrato/qinqin:v0.5.0` (also `latest`). Documentation:
https://github.com/Elaktrato/qinqin/wiki

## Upgrading from v0.4.x

- **No database migrations** in this release; back up the volume as usual before you upgrade.
- **Run `qinqin seed` once** (`docker compose exec qinqin qinqin seed`, or the Portainer console) to install the new
  Chinese and Japanese grammar packs. The first-start auto-seed only runs on a fresh instance, so it does not add them
  to an existing one. The old ten-lesson `zh-grammar-demo` pack stays installed until you uninstall it in
  Settings → Data.
- Cached copies of the installed app pick up the new version on their next load. Until then, an old tab counts both
  the old and the new review when a grade is changed (see "Change a grade" below).

## What's new

**Study**
- **Change a misclicked grade.** In a session, **Back** (or the **Z** key) returns to the card you just graded; pick
  another grade to replace it, for up to the last 20 cards. The old grade then counts nowhere: scheduling, XP, streaks,
  limits, goals, milestones, the weekly summary, Stats, the heatmap, the optimizer and backups (#131).
- **Card types are mixed in every session**, with a fresh mix each session: two cards of one word are kept apart, a
  new word comes first as Recognise, and the most overdue reviews stay near the front. "Order of card types: Mixed /
  Grouped by type" is in the session setup (#132).

**Reading and dictionary**
- **Japanese lemmatizer.** The reader and lookup recognise inflected and contracted forms as their dictionary word:
  している, しました, しちゃった, 食べてしまった, 読んでる, 食べないでください, 行ってはいけません, 帰ってもいい,
  誤解されかねない. Grammar expressions such as に基づいて or によれば stay whole. Furigana sits over the kanji only.
  The engine is language-neutral and driven by rule tables, so other languages can follow (#165, #177).
- **Grammar lessons for every level:** Chinese ≈ HSK 1 to 7–9 (386 lessons) and Japanese ≈ JLPT N5 to N1
  (265 lessons), written for qinqin under CC0 1.0. They have not been reviewed by a native speaker yet (#164).
- **Example sentences from Tatoeba:** the tooling is ready (pinned export, licence filter, readings, credits); the
  sentence packs themselves are not in this release (#133).

**The app**
- **Phone bottom bar:** on phones the sections you choose sit in a bar at the bottom (at most five, or four and
  More); the side menu stays available as an option in Settings → Layout (#188).
- **Activity heatmap** shows 52 weeks with compact filters and a This workspace / All workspaces switch (#160);
  a larger logo (#161).
- **Offline study fixed:** the installed app now saves the study pages and the review history it needs offline
  (they were sent as not storable since v0.4.0) (#110).

**Server and API**
- `GET /api/events` is paged when you send `limit` or `page`; without them the old full answer is still sent, now
  streamed (#110).
- Review events may carry `replaces` (the changed-grade rule, `docs/contracts/events/review-replace.json`);
  `/api/segment` matches carry `stem`.

**Documentation**
- The user, admin and API guide is now the GitHub wiki, published from `docs/wiki/` on every push to `main`; the
  README is a short front page (#80).

## Known limits

- Nothing in this release was checked in a real browser or on a phone before tagging; the hand-check lists are in
  `docs/superpowers/handoffs/2026-11-22-study-batch-handoff.md` and `2026-11-22-phone-bottom-bar.md`.
- Japanese without a frequency pack: bare して reads as the noun 仕手 and されている as される; the bundled Jiten
  pack (installed by default) resolves both to する.
- The Tatoeba sentence packs still have to be built (#133); lemmatizer rule tables as data packs are planned (#176).
- The Android app is not part of this release.
