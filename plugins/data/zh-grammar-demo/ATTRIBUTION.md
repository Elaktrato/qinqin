# Chinese grammar demo (A1)

Ten short grammar lessons for beginners (是, 不, 吗, 很, 的, 也/都, 个, 在, 有/没有, 了),
with 40 example sentences, readings, translations and exercise answers.

- Author: written for qinqin by the project. The explanations, example
  sentences, readings and the wrong answers used in the exercises are
  original; nothing was copied or adapted from a textbook, a syllabus or
  another grammar resource.
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication),
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. See `LICENSE`.
- Levels: "A1 · Part 1/2" are the author's own estimates; the link to
  "HSK 1" is only a rough orientation and is not taken from the HSK syllabus.
- Status: demo content. It has NOT been reviewed by a native speaker; errors
  are possible and corrections are welcome.
- Pinyin: tone marks as in the HSK word list pack (`hsk-words`): 不 and 一 are
  written as they are spoken (bú shì, yí ge), third-tone sandhi is not marked
  (nǐ hǎo), neutral tones are unmarked.
