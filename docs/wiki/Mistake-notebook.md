# Mistake notebook

The **mistake notebook** (`/notebook`) gathers what you got wrong in the current workspace into one list. Open it from
the Decks page, the Practice page or the Grammar page (**Mistake notebook**).

## What goes in

- A **flashcard** you answered **Again** (in a study session, or a course lesson's first review).
- A **drill item** you answered wrong (tone pairs, conjugation, measure words, counters, look-alike characters).
- A **grammar point** whose lesson or review run scored below 80 %.
- A **course lesson** whose check scored below 80 %, a **unit test** or a **section exam** you did not pass. A test
  taken early to skip ahead, and a placement exam ("Find your place"), are not mistakes.

The list is grouped into words, characters, tones, grammar, and course lessons and tests, newest mistake first. Each
line says what went wrong the last time, how many mistakes it has had since it came in, and a link: the word's page,
the drill, the grammar lesson, the course lesson or test.

## What takes it out

- **Three right answers in a row.** Every right answer after the last mistake counts ("1 of 3 right in a row"); a new
  mistake starts the count again. For a card, Hard, Good and Easy are right; for a grammar run or a course lesson, 80 %
  or more; for a test, a pass.
- **Remove** takes one item out by hand; **Clear the notebook** takes everything out. A new mistake brings an item back.

Clearing changes only the notebook: your answers, schedules, streak and stats stay as they are.

## Practise my mistakes

- The notebook's **drill items** come in one round (`/notebook/practise`), each with its own drill's question.
- Its **cards that are due now** start a study session of just those cards (**Review the due cards**). A card is never
  asked before it is due: a card you answered Again comes back within minutes anyway, and the others come back in
  your reviews.
- **Grammar points and course tests** open from their line: run the lesson or take the test again.

Your answers there are ordinary answers: they count for your schedules, XP, the streak and the heatmap, and for the
notebook's three in a row.

## Your data

The notebook is worked out on your device from your study history, like your schedules. The only thing it saves is
what you removed or cleared, as small events in your study history: per workspace, synced between your devices, and
in your backups. Rules for other clients: `docs/contracts/notebook/`.
