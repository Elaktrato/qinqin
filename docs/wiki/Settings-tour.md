# Settings tour

Settings is in the footer of every signed-in page. The open tab is part of the address (`/settings?tab=home`), so you can bookmark or share a tab. Tabs are Workspaces, Home, Study, Plugins, AI and Account for everyone, and Data and Admin for administrators.

## Workspaces

Create, rename and delete workspaces ([Workspaces and languages](Workspaces-and-languages)); choose which dictionaries a workspace hides; and two audio options for the current workspace (pronunciation buttons on entries and, after the answer, on study cards).

## Home

Chooses and orders the home-page widgets. Each widget has an "On the home page" switch and **Move up** / **Move down** buttons (or drag a row by its handle). A preview shows the result and **Reset to default** restores the default layout (activity, then flashcards). Changes save immediately to your account, so every device and workspace gets them. A widget whose plugin is disabled keeps its place and returns when the plugin is enabled again; one that does not fit the active workspace's language is marked "Not available for <language>".

## Study

- **Time zone.** Every "day" in qinqin (heatmap, streaks, daily XP bonus, reading totals, vacation, daily limits) is your local day. The zone is stored on the server so every client agrees. The first time you sign in, the web app sends your browser's zone if your account has none. If your device zone later differs, Settings offers "Use <zone> from now on"; nothing changes by itself. The first zone you ever get applies to your whole history; later changes apply from the moment you save. Without a zone, days are UTC.
- **Daily limits** and **Vacation** ([Decks, limits and vacation](Flashcards-decks-limits-vacation)).
- A link to Scheduling ([Scheduling and FSRS](Scheduling-and-FSRS)).

## Plugins

Switch feature plugins on or off (flashcards, Reader, Journal and others). Each plugin lists the permissions it declared and the third-party material it uses. Switching a plugin off hides its pages and widgets.

## AI

Providers, keys and model per provider, the active provider, and this hour's usage ([Journal and AI providers](Journal-and-AI)). Administrators also register custom endpoints here.

## Account

Profile (email, if the server asks for one), password, two-step sign-in, signed-in devices, "Your data" (export and restore), the app and offline section, and Delete account. See [Accounts and sign-in](Accounts-and-sign-in), [Your data](Your-data) and [Install as an app and study offline](Install-and-offline).

## Data (administrators)

Install dictionary and level packs, register or remove languages ([Data and licences](Data-and-licences)).

## Admin (administrators)

Sections Users, Invites, Email, Security and Backup (`/settings?tab=admin&section=users` and so on). See [Users, registration and invites](Admin-users-and-registration), [Email, two-step sign-in and security](Admin-email-and-security) and [Backup, recovery and the command line](Admin-backup-and-recovery).
