# qinqin v0.8.0

The beginner experience, and the first release with an Android app. qinqin is now an app for your phone (the web app
of your own server in a native shell), with study reminders and a version check, and a way in for people who are new
to Chinese or Japanese: placement, a writing-system trainer, Starter mode, optional hints and pictures, and example
sentences from Tatoeba. There is no separate v0.7.0 release: its work ships here, and the changes from both are
listed below.

The APK is for sideloading and comes with the GitHub release; the image is `ghcr.io/elaktrato/qinqin:v0.8.0` (`latest`
follows `main`, not a release: pin the version tag). Documentation: https://github.com/Elaktrato/qinqin/wiki

## What's new

**The Android app** ([Android](https://github.com/Elaktrato/qinqin/wiki/Android))
- The qinqin web app in a native shell. You type the address of **your own** server on the first screen (it checks
  that it is qinqin and shows its version) and the app opens that server's web app, so a server update reaches the
  phone with no app update. Change server from the account menu or the launcher shortcut.
- **Offline study** works as in the browser, through the web app's service worker; reviews graded offline are sent
  when you are back online. It needs an **https** address.
- **Phone features:** device text-to-speech voice, camera and photo picker for the journal, saving backups through
  Android's save screen, share out and share in ("Look up in qinqin"), screen kept on while studying, vibration on
  grading, theme-coloured status and navigation bars, the back gesture, and links to other sites open in your browser.
- Only your server's address gets these native features.

**Study reminders** ([Reminders](https://github.com/Elaktrato/qinqin/wiki/Reminders))
- Optional and off until you turn them on (Settings, Notifications): cards due, daily goal not met, streak at
  risk, at times you choose, with quiet hours. Channels: **device notifications in the Android app**, **email**
  and **web push** for the browser or installed web app. Reminders carry counts only, never what you study.

**Version and update check** ([Updates](https://github.com/Elaktrato/qinqin/wiki/Admin-updates))
- `GET /api/version` tells an app which server it talks to. Administrators see the installed version and whether a
  newer release exists under Settings, Admin, Updates (it never updates anything, and contacts GitHub only when you
  look or press **Check now**).

**Getting started** ([Getting started](https://github.com/Elaktrato/qinqin/wiki/Getting-started))
- **"I already know this":** declare a level range (an HSK or JLPT level) as known. Known marks are kept apart from
  mastery, so they never count as study progress, and a whole declaration can be undone in one step.
- **Placement test:** a short adaptive test that places you in a level, using your own answers (self-report). It is
  seeded, so the same answers give the same result.
- **Level check** (experimental): estimates how ready you are for an HSK or JLPT level, from your own data plus a
  sample of the rest. Under Settings, Study, and offered quietly on Home. Its thresholds are a first guess, not
  validated against exam results.

**Starter mode** ([Flashcards](https://github.com/Elaktrato/qinqin/wiki/Flashcards-study-sessions))
- A base vocabulary that reveals the script word by word: a picture with the romanisation, then the kana (or the
  characters with furigana or pinyin), then the normal card. A word moves on after a count of spaced successes.
- The lists are small (55 Japanese and 47 Chinese words) and **not yet reviewed by a native speaker**.

**Writing-system trainer** ([Scripts trainer](https://github.com/Elaktrato/qinqin/wiki/Scripts-trainer))
- Charts and practice for kana, Hangul and Cyrillic: recognise, recall, read and type, a Hangul syllable builder,
  and a **Write** mode for hiragana and katakana with the writing pad. Progress comes from your study events.
- Korean and Russian are new language profiles.

**Hints and visual aids**
- **Hints on every card:** one Hint button (or the H key; Alt+H inside a text field). Per card kind you choose
  Always, As a hint, or Never. A hint lowers the grade suggestion by one step.
- **Visual aids**, each with its own switch and set per workspace: pictures for words, tone colours (with a separate
  contour cue under each syllable) and character anatomy.
- **Pictures** come from the server's `images` pack (OpenMoji and Mulberry). They are drawn from vetted SVG only and
  need the server (no offline pictures yet). About one picture in ten may be wrong or doubtful.

**Data**
- **Example sentences:** about 32,800 Chinese and 55,900 Japanese sentences from Tatoeba, bundled. Each example shows
  who wrote it and links to the sentence on Tatoeba, as the licence requires.
- **Japanese stroke order:** the `kanjivg-strokes` pack (kana, Japanese kanji forms) from KanjiVG; Japanese now
  shows Japanese forms where they differ from Chinese.

**Study and reading (from the Android work)**
- The study modal: the study setup is saved per workspace on your account and used on every device.
- Reader: tap a word for a sticky lookup, with a phone sheet that keeps the word visible; tap a highlight to look it
  up. Typing cards, search and the journal no longer act on Enter while a Chinese or Japanese IME is composing.
- Thin, themed scrollbars.

**Also in this release:** the Android emulator tests run more reliably, the compose health check waits up to 600 s for
the first start, and a server behind your own certificate authority (Caddy's internal CA, a home-lab or headscale
setup) works once you install that CA on the phone.

**Licensing**
- The code is under the MIT licence; content the project writes (grammar packs, the lemmatizer rule table, the qinqin
  themes) is under CC BY 4.0, and so are the script charts and the Starter lists. Bundled data keeps its own licences
  (`docs/sources.md`): KanjiVG (stroke data and character anatomy) is CC BY-SA 3.0, Tatoeba is CC BY 2.0 FR, OpenMoji
  and Mulberry are CC BY-SA 4.0. Each share-alike pack stays its own data pack, served by the server and never built
  into the web app or the APK. Notices: `docs/sources.md` and each pack's `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Installing the Android app

1. Download `qinqin-0.8.0.apk` and `qinqin-0.8.0.apk.sha256` from the release. The APK is built and signed by the
   project owner on their own machine and attached by hand; it is not built by GitHub.
2. `sha256sum --check qinqin-0.8.0.apk.sha256` must print `qinqin-0.8.0.apk: OK`. This shows the file is intact, not
   who made it. To check the signature: `apksigner verify --print-certs qinqin-0.8.0.apk` and compare the SHA-256
   certificate digest with the fingerprint:
   `dd404b098b56a59931fbad957df8b44d5f5cf650297ac986e5b745ac9d266bbd`.
3. Copy it to the phone, allow "Install unknown apps" for your file manager, open it, and enter your server address.
4. A server with a certificate from your own certificate authority: install that CA's **root** certificate on the
   phone first (Settings, Security, Encryption and credentials, Install a certificate, CA certificate). See the wiki
   page Android.

Updating: install a newer APK over it (same signing key, higher version); your address and data stay. The new features
come from your server, so a server update reaches the phone with no app update.

## Upgrading from v0.6.0

- **Back up first.** Four new migrations run at the first start: **051** (update-check settings), **052** (reminders:
  facts, push subscriptions, delivery log, the sealed push key), **053** (images packs) and **054** (Tatoeba credit
  columns). Migrations are one-way; going back needs your backup.
- **Install the new packs.** Auto-seed runs only on a fresh instance, so an existing server gets the Tatoeba
  sentences, the images packs and the KanjiVG strokes by running `qinqin seed` (or the admin importer); the sentences
  data package is rebuilt in **format 2** (credit per sentence; there is no compatibility with format 1 packs).
- **Contract version 13** (`GET /api/version`): see `docs/contracts/CHANGELOG.md` (bridge features, reminder and push
  routes, declarations on known marks, the scripts feature, hints, `images` pack routes, visual aids, Starter sets,
  per-sentence credit, the stroke `lang` parameter). The bridge is level 1 with the features `files`, `haptics`,
  `keep-awake`, `local-notifications`, `share`, `status-bar`, `tts`. An older app against this server warns that it
  lacks features and still opens.
- **`QINQIN_SECRET`:** web push needs it (the push key is kept sealed with it); without it the push switch says it is
  not available. Email reminders need a configured mail server and a verified address. Optional
  `QINQIN_VAPID_SUBJECT`.
- **Reverse proxy:** see [Reverse proxy](https://github.com/Elaktrato/qinqin/wiki/Admin-proxy-and-rate-limiting#the-android-app) for what the app needs.
- **First start takes longer:** about 2.5 minutes on a busy machine; the compose health check now allows 600 s.
- **Docker build:** by default the image downloads about 56 MB of pinned picture and stroke sources;
  `--build-arg WITH_IMAGES=0` and `WITH_KANJIVG=0` skip them.
- **Study setup and settings:** each workspace starts once from the defaults for the study setup; what a browser
  remembered before is not carried over. The hint, visual-aid and Starter choices are new workspace settings.

## Known limits

- **Sideload only.** There is no Play Store or F-Droid listing; stores come after v1.0.
- **Plain http has no offline start:** a service worker needs https, so over http the app shows its first screen with
  **Try again** when the server is unreachable.
- **Not every feature has been checked by hand on a phone or in a browser:** CI covers logic, the web app and an
  Android emulator. Unchecked by hand: picture sizing and the dark/forced-colours themes, tone colour and contour
  layout, the KanjiVG outlines and the pen matcher on touch, the Starter, placement and level-check flows, Korean and
  Japanese IME input in type mode, and device voices for Japanese, Korean and Russian.
- Starter lists, example words and romanisations were written without native-speaker review (55 Japanese and 47
  Chinese words, against the intended 100-300).
- Pictures need the server (there is no offline picture package), and the picture matching is about 90 % right.
- Hangul and Cyrillic have no Write mode (KanjiVG has no data for them).
- Android's WebView must be recent enough; the app says so and refuses a server otherwise.
- Not in this release: Mandarin recordings (#237). They come later, after v1.0.
