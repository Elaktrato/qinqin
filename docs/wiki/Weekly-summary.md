# Weekly summary

The weekly summary shows what you did in one workspace over one week, Monday to Sunday, compared with the week before. It is in the app only: there is no email and nothing is sent anywhere. The numbers are worked out from what you did each time you open it; nothing is stored.

Open it under **Stats**, tab **Week** (`/stats/week`), or from the **This week** widget on the home page (its link **See the week**).

## Which week

A week is Monday to Sunday in your own days: your time zone, as set in [Settings tour](Settings-tour), not UTC dates. A review at 23:30 on a Monday in Berlin belongs to that Monday. A week in which the clocks change still has seven days. If you moved across the date line and a date never happened for you, that day is shown as "no such day" and is not counted.

**Previous week** and **Next week** step through the weeks. You can go back 52 weeks. The current week says **In progress**; its days after today are shown as upcoming and are not counted.

## What the numbers mean

| Row | Counts |
|---|---|
| Reviews | every flashcard review you graded (the same count as the heatmap and the daily goal) |
| New cards | cards reviewed for the first time that week |
| Flashcard study time | time spent answering cards, at most 60 seconds per card |
| Reading | minutes read in the Reader |
| Journal entries | journal entries written |
| Other activity | each plugin activity that was counted this week or last week |
| Days active | days on which you did anything that counts (vacation days are left out, see below) |
| Streak | your review streak at the end of the week (today for the current week) |
| Words reaching mature | words whose flashcard became mature that week: in review with a stability of 21 days or more, as on the decks view. A word that became mature and slipped back in the same week does not count |
| Characters learned | Chinese and Japanese workspaces only: characters whose score reached 80 of 100 that week, as on the character map. This row appears a moment after the others ("Counting characters…") |
| Daily goal | the days on which you reached your [daily goal](Daily-goals) out of the days it counted; today counts once its goal is reached |

## Compared with last week

Each number says how it compares with the week before: "12 more than last week", "3 fewer than last week" or "Same as last week". While the week is in progress it compares with last week **up to the same weekday** ("Compared with Monday to Wednesday last week"), so a Wednesday is not compared with a whole week. With nothing in the week before (your first week, or after a break) it says "Nothing last week to compare". The arrows are decoration; the words say the same.

## Vacation days

A [vacation](Flashcards-decks-limits-vacation) day is marked in the day strip and is neutral: it does not count for or against **Days active** or the daily goal, and it never breaks the streak. Anything you did on a vacation day still counts in the totals. A week that was vacation from start to end says **On vacation all week**.

## The day strip

Seven boxes, Monday to Sunday: active (a tick, filled), not active (a dot), vacation (V, dashed), upcoming (dotted) and "no such day" (a dash). Each box has its full description as a tooltip and for screen readers, for example "Monday 16 November: active, 42 reviews".

## Offline

Without a connection the summary still shows what this device knows: new cards, study time, the streak and mature words. Reviews, reading, journal entries and plugin activity say "Not available offline"; so does **Characters learned** when the language's character list was not loaded yet in this session. **Retry** loads them again. Days active then counts flashcard reviews only and says so.

## The home widget

**This week** shows the current week so far: reviews, new cards, study time (flashcards and reading together), days active with the day strip and the streak, each compared with last week up to the same weekday. New accounts have it on the home page after the daily goal; an existing home layout is not changed (add it under Settings, Home).
