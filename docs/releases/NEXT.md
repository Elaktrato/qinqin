# qinqin: next release

Notes collected for the next release; they move into its `vX.Y.Z.md` when it is cut.

## Upgrading

- **Study setup** (the study modal): the study setup is now saved per workspace on your account and used on every
  device. What a browser remembered before (the last card types started in a workspace and the Reverse/Cloze
  checkboxes unchecked on the study page, the setup of an open tab) is not carried over: each workspace starts
  from the defaults (every deck, Vocab, Reverse and Cloze as your decks switch them on) once.
