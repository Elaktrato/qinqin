# Chinese grammar — HSK coverage

Topic coverage follows the grammar outline (语法大纲) of the 《HSK 考试大纲》 / Syllabus for the
Chinese Proficiency Test, CLEC/CTI, 2025-11 (effective 2026-07). The outline is used as a topic
index only: the lesson grouping and order, explanations, examples and exercises are qinqin’s own,
and no syllabus text is reproduced. The syllabus grants no reuse licence.
